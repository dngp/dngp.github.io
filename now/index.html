Research

We have pronounced ca 10 open problems in our articles (and my thesis); we are now working towards solving them and other problems.

  • Generalisation of Kirillov's problem to N-ary SH-Lie algebras: attainability of our bounds, slower growth.
  • Topological aspects of Wronskian N-ary SH-Lie algebras.
  • Factorisation of generalised Vandermonde determinants: construction of totally antisymmetric N-ary differential operators.
  • Generalisations of Skyrmions, related topological constructions, higher order Wronskians.
